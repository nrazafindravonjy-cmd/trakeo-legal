Privacy policy

Version of September 29, 2026

Trakeo helps you plan your budget. Your financial data stays on your phone by default: the online account is optional, and nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or sent to an external artificial intelligence.

Who is responsible for your data

Nirintsoa Patrick Razafindravonjy, a private individual publishing Trakeo on a non-professional basis.

Contact for any question about your data: n.razafindravonjy@gmail.com. Data protection officer or contact: n.razafindravonjy@gmail.com.

The data we process

  • On your phone only (without an account): income and payment dates, balance, expenses and income you record, merchants and notes, lifestyle profile (city, housing, transport, habits), savings goals, settings. This data is encrypted on the device.
  • Receipt photos: read on your phone to extract the amount, date and merchant. The photo and the recognised text never leave your phone.
  • If you create an account: your email address (or the identifier provided by Google or Apple if you sign in with them) and a copy of your Trakeo data, encrypted in transit, for backup and syncing between your devices.
  • Usage measurement: only if you turn it on. It covers anonymous usage events only (for example "goal created"), never an amount, merchant or free text. In this version, no usage data is sent.

We do not collect your banking credentials: Trakeo does not connect to any bank.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the service (calculations, forecasts, goals, reminders, and backup and sync if you create an account): performance of the contract between us, that is the terms of use (GDPR article 6.1.b).
  • Usage measurement: your consent (article 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time in Profile → Settings → Privacy. Refusing changes nothing in how the app works.
  • Account security (server sign-in logs): our legitimate interest in protecting the service and your data (article 6.1.f).

Automated decisions

The "safe to spend" amount and the forecasts are computed on your phone. They are estimates to help you decide, not decisions that produce legal effects or significantly affect you (GDPR article 22). No credit, offer or marketing profile results from them.

Who receives your data

No one other than us and our technical processors, and only if you create an account:

  • Account and sync hosting: Supabase, Inc., 970 Toa Payoh North #07-04, Singapore 318992 (support@supabase.com). Data is hosted in the European Union.
  • Sign-in with Google or Apple (if you choose it): Google or Apple receive the sign-in request under their own policies.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with any advertiser.

Transfers outside the European Union

Your Trakeo data is hosted in the European Union. Some providers (for example Google or Apple for sign-in, or our host's sub-processors) may process data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • On the server (with an account): as long as your account exists. Deleting your account immediately erases your account and all your synced data. The host keeps no automatic backup of your data.
  • Security logs: according to the host's retention period, at most 12 months.

Security

Data encrypted on the device, encrypted connections (TLS) to the server, server access limited to your own data, app lock by PIN or biometrics, and amounts hidden in the app switcher.

Your rights

At any time you can:

  • access your data and take it with you in a reusable format: Profile → Settings → Privacy → Export my data;
  • correct it: every piece of information can be edited in the app;
  • erase it: on this phone, or everywhere by deleting your account;
  • restrict or object to processing, and withdraw your consent to usage measurement;
  • set instructions on what happens to your data after your death.

For any request, email us at n.razafindravonjy@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07), or with the authority of your EU country of residence.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: requested only when you tap "Take a photo".
  • Notifications: offered after you create your first goal. They are prepared on your phone; no server sends them to you.

No cookies or advertising trackers are used.

Minimum age

Trakeo is intended for people aged 15 and over. If you are under 18, we recommend talking about it with your parents or legal guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes significantly, the app tells you and asks you to accept it again. The version date is at the top of this document.
