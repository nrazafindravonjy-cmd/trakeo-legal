Accessibility statement

Version of September 29, 2026

Nirintsoa Patrick Razafindravonjy is committed to making Trakeo accessible, in line with EU Directive 2019/882 on the accessibility requirements for products and services, and with the WCAG 2.1 level AA and EN 301 549 standards.

Conformance status

Trakeo has not yet had an external accessibility audit. It is partially conformant: the points below are checked automatically for every release; the rest remains to be audited.

What is in place

  • Colour contrast checked automatically, in light and dark themes.
  • Text can be enlarged up to 200% without loss of information.
  • Screen reader labels (VoiceOver, TalkBack) on buttons, amounts and the main charts.
  • Tap targets of at least 48 × 48 points.
  • The risk level is always written out, never shown by colour alone.
  • Decorative animations (confetti) are turned off when your phone asks for reduced motion.
  • Haptic feedback can be turned off in the settings.

Known limitations

  • Some charts can be hard to explore with a screen reader; their main values are also given as text.
  • Receipt reading relies on a photo; manual entry is always available.

Contact us

If you have difficulty accessing any content or feature, email us at n.razafindravonjy@gmail.com: we will reply and offer an alternative.

If you do not get a satisfactory answer, you can contact the French Défenseur des droits (www.defenseurdesdroits.fr) or the competent authority in your country.
