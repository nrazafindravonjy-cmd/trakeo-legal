Delete your Trakeo account

Trakeo is a budgeting app for students, published by Nirintsoa Patrick Razafindravonjy. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, free of charge.

In the app (immediate)

  • Open Profile → Settings → Privacy and data.
  • Tap Delete my account, then Delete everything.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without the app

Email n.razafindravonjy@gmail.com from your account’s email address and ask for deletion. We delete the account within 30 days at the latest and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, sign-in identifiers).
  • All your synced data: income, expenses, goals, profile.
  • The host keeps no automatic backup of your data.

What may be kept

  • The host’s security logs (IP address, sign-in events), for at most 12 months, to protect the service.
  • Our email exchanges for a support request, for 3 years after the last exchange.

Data stored only on your phone (no-account mode) is erased from within the app, or by uninstalling it.
